    What changes between the inactive and the active model?

    23,025 of 213,456 contact pairs (10.8%) pass their receptor's threshold.

    Each receptor has its own threshold

    One dot per receptor.

    Per receptor, contact pairs with |ΔRRCS| at or above mean + SD of |ΔRRCS| (at least 0.2), the threshold each report uses.

    Above-threshold pairs per 100 pairs: the receptor's above-threshold contact pairs divided by all its contact pairs, times 100.

    Which positions change in most receptors?

    3.50x50 (R of DRY) is a CFR in 274 of 283 receptors.

    What are Core Functional Residues (CFRs)?
    • CFR (Core Functional Residue): a residue in at least one above-threshold contact of its receptor.
    • 368 recurrent positions: generic positions that are CFRs in three or more receptors.
    • 50 top-ranked positions: the highest CFR score, which weighs how many receptors have a CFR at the position together with the mean |ΔRRCS| of those contacts. They are highlighted across the site.

    Cross-receptor recurrence analyses are limited to residues assigned GPCRdb generic numbers.

    Definitions, scope and the activation reference

    Where these cross-receptor numbers come from. Cross-receptor recurrence analyses are limited to residues assigned GPCRdb generic numbers. Residues without one, largely in loops and terminal regions, cannot be aligned to a shared position across receptors and take no part in these statistics. Recurrence is counted on the full GPCRdb residue maps of all 283 receptors. The list of 287 human Class A receptors also names GPR26, LGR5, OPSX and RXFP1, which lack a complete active and inactive model pair and are not in the database.

    Models
    Each of the 283 receptors is compared between its active and inactive predicted models from GPCRdb.
    ΔRRCS
    RRCS (residue-residue contact score) of a residue contact pair in the active model minus that in the inactive model. Positive: stronger in the active model.
    Threshold
    Per receptor: the mean plus one standard deviation of its |ΔRRCS| values. A pair at or above it is above threshold.
    CFR
    Core Functional Residue: a residue in at least one above-threshold contact of its receptor.
    Positions
    Compared across receptors by GPCRdb generic number. A recurrent position is a CFR in three or more receptors (368 positions); the 50 top-ranked positions have the highest CFR score.
    Decisive call
    An AlphaMissense prediction that is predicted pathogenic or predicted benign (not ambiguous). AlphaMissense classes are predictions, not clinical evidence; conservation is an observation across species.

    Known activation positions (39): Zhou et al. 2019 common activation pathway, plus D2.50, DRY, PIF, CWxP, NPxxY, the sodium pocket and 6.30. 3.50x50, 6.48x48, 7.53x53, 3.49x49, 5.58x58, 6.44x44, 8.50x50, 7.54x54, 8.51x51, 7.45x45, 6.30x30, 7.55x55, 3.40x40, 7.49x49, 7.50x50, 3.43x43, 2.43x43, 2.50x50, 6.37x37, 5.62x62, 5.51x51, 6.40x40, 7.51x51, 3.53x53, 6.47x47, 7.46x46, 6.41x41, 3.51x51, 1.49x49, 3.46x46, 3.39x39, 1.53x53, 7.52x52, 6.49x49, 5.57x57, 2.46x46, 5.55x55, 5.50x50, 6.50x50.
