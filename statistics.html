    Chart notes
    • Thresholds Include pairs at or above the receptor mean |ΔRRCS| plus one standard deviation, with a 0.2 minimum.
    • Contact distribution Bin width is one ΔRRCS unit.
    • Helix pairs Direction: ≥10 carriers, absent pairs zero, agreement among carriers. Omit within-helix pairs. Magnitude: all TM1–TM7 contacts (≥50/interface).
    • Segments Each endpoint counts once toward its residue’s segment, including contacts below threshold.
    • Scaffold GPCRdb ADRB2 layout spans six residues before TM1 through eight after H8. Bands match variant recurrence groups.
    • CFR ranking 9 of 16 activation motif positions rank in the top 50.
    • Recovery Each structure position takes its highest generic CFR score. Chance equals rank × known/eligible positions.
    • Partners Count each receptor once per exact above-threshold pair of generic-numbered CFRs.
    • CFR matrix Rows sort by CFR count within ligand type. Direction uses summed ΔRRCS. Phones show ligand-group shares.
    • AlphaMissense Missense sites: contact |ΔRRCS| ≥3.0. Exclude 1,260 unclassified substitutions across 49 receptors (483 at top 50). Predictions only.
    • Recurrence bands Modeled contacts cover 15,519 of 15,524 decisive calls. The other 5 lack modeled contacts.
    • Conservation Average cross-species scores over numbered, scored residues. CFR ranking uses contact data alone.
    • Conservation quintiles Of 15,519 decisive calls with modeled contacts, 12 lack residue conservation scores.
    • Position variants Pool numbered receptors. Count each receptor, position, reference and alternate amino acid combination once.
    • Explorer Similarity compares signed top-50 fingerprints by cosine. Total |ΔRRCS| grows with receptor size.
