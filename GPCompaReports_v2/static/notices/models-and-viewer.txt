GPCompaRe database: notices for the 3D view

Structure models
----------------
The 3D view on each receptor report shows the receptor's active and inactive
models from GPCRdb (https://gpcrdb.org), 2024-05-15 build: AlphaFold-Multistate,
with plain AlphaFold2 for some states (165 inactive and 26 active models of the
283 receptors). Each report names the method of its two models.

  Changes:   none to the coordinates. The files under /models/ are the GPCRdb PDB
             files byte for byte, compressed with gzip (file name
             <receptor>_<state>.pdb.gz, for example par2_human_active.pdb.gz).
             Residue numbers follow the models.

Viewer
------
The 3D view uses the pair viewer of GPCompaRe Studio (/studio/embed/), which
bundles Mol* (https://molstar.org, MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2017 - now Mol*
contributors) and the packages it depends on. The licence text and the list of
packages are in /studio/embed/NOTICE.txt and /studio/embed/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.

  Sehnal D, Bittrich S, Deshpande M, et al. Mol* Viewer: modern web app and
  plugin for visualizing 3D structures and large-scale data.
  Nucleic Acids Res 49:W431-W437 (2021). https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkab314

ClinVar
-------
The ClinVar section lists records from NCBI ClinVar (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/),
release 2026-10-11: germline pathogenic, likely pathogenic and pathogenic/likely
pathogenic single-residue missense records.
