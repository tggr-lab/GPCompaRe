Privacy

The GPCompaRe database is a set of static pages. It has no user accounts and sets no cookies. These are the outside services a visit can reach, and what each one receives.

ServiceWhenWhat it receives
GitHub Pages Every page, because it hosts the site What any web server receives with a request: your IP address, your browser's user agent and the page you asked for.
GoatCounter Every page, to count visits The page you visited, the page you came from, your browser's user agent, screen size and your IP address. GoatCounter is cookie-free; by its own account it keeps no IP addresses and records only aggregate counts (pages, referrers, browsers, screen sizes and countries).
Plotly CDN (cdn.plot.ly) Receptor reports and the Statistics page, which load the Plotly charting library from it A file request: your IP address, your browser's user agent and the address of the page that asked for the file.
Formspree Only when you send the contact form Everything you type into the form (name, email, institution, enquiry type, receptor, page address and message), plus your IP address and browser details. Formspree forwards the message to the TGGR Laboratory by email.

Kept in your browser only

Your display preferences (light or dark theme, the compact view on reports) and any game high score are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and clearing site data removes them.

GPCompaRe Studio

GPCompaRe Studio runs in your browser. Files you open are read on your computer and are not uploaded. It loads no analytics and no charting library from elsewhere. It contacts RCSB or the AlphaFold Protein Structure Database only when you ask it to fetch a structure. It keeps your display preferences and an offline copy of itself in your browser.

3D view on reports

The 3D view on a receptor report loads nothing until you press Show 3D view. It then loads the viewer and the receptor's two model files, all from this site. No third party is contacted, and nothing about the structure you look at is sent anywhere. Credits for the models and the viewer are on the report and in the notices. The links to GPCRdb, ClinVar and other databases on a report are ordinary links and send nothing until you follow them.

Not loaded from elsewhere

The heading font, Source Serif 4, is served from this site (SIL Open Font License, licence text), so no font service is contacted.
