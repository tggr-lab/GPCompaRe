Browse Class A GPCRs

Search and filter receptor reports. Open a receptor to inspect its contact changes, residue-level summaries, variants, and downloadable tables.

Variant: a gene or receptor name with a protein change, such as ADRB2 p.Arg16Gly or PAR2 M159I; an rsID; or a gnomAD variant ID such as 5-76833084-G-A. Results come from each receptor's variant table.

Position: a GPCRdb generic number such as 6.48x48, 6.48 or 3.50x50 lists every receptor with a residue there, using the numbering of the snake plots.

Column guide: Contacts: total residue-residue contact pairs detected (within 4.63 Å in either the active or the inactive state). Above threshold: contact pairs whose |ΔRRCS| exceeds the per-receptor ΔRRCS threshold. Total |ΔRRCS|: sum of absolute ΔRRCS across all contacts. Receptor-level summary of total contact-score redistribution between active and inactive models. Contact-site variants: gnomAD missense variants at residues that take part in at least one contact pair with |ΔRRCS| ≥ 3.0 (a fixed cutoff, not the per-receptor threshold).
