    Definitions, scope and the activation reference

    Where these cross-receptor numbers come from. Cross-receptor recurrence analyses are limited to residues assigned GPCRdb generic numbers. Residues without one, largely in loops and terminal regions, cannot be aligned to a shared position across receptors and take no part in these statistics. Recurrence is counted on the full GPCRdb residue maps of all {{ total_gpcrs }} receptors.{% if exclusions %} The list of {{ exclusions.listed }} human Class A receptors also names {{ exclusions.names }}, which lack a complete active and inactive model pair and are not in the database.{% endif %}

    Models
    Each of the {{ total_gpcrs }} receptors is compared between its active and inactive predicted models from GPCRdb.
    ΔRRCS
    RRCS (residue-residue contact score) of a residue contact pair in the active model minus that in the inactive model. Positive: stronger in the active model.
    Threshold
    Per receptor: the mean plus one standard deviation of its |ΔRRCS| values. A pair at or above it is above threshold.
    CFR
    Core Functional Residue: a residue in at least one above-threshold contact of its receptor.
    Positions
    Compared across receptors by GPCRdb generic number. A recurrent position is a CFR in three or more receptors ({{ cfr_total_positions }} positions); the {{ cfr_top_n }} top-ranked positions have the highest CFR score.
    Decisive call
    An AlphaMissense prediction that is predicted pathogenic or predicted benign (not ambiguous). AlphaMissense classes are predictions, not clinical evidence; conservation is an observation across species.
